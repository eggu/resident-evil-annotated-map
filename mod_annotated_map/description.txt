Annotated Map: Survivor's Notes 1.0.0
For Classic REbirth 1.1.4 (GOG Biohazard.exe).

Map overlay: room names, typewriters/item boxes, locked doors and their keys, key items, files/maps, puzzles, common items.
M (or pad RB): full-screen map. Map hotkeys: 0 all, 1 rooms, 2 locks, 3 key items, 4 files, 5 puzzles, 6 common items,
7 full screen / status map, 8 visited / revealed / all rooms, 9 English / Korean.
Settings: mod_annotated_map/RE1.AnnotatedMap.ini (created on first run).
